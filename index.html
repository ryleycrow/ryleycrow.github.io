Welcome

Hi, I'm Ryley Crow, a PhD candidate at George Washington University. I study the immune system in non-mammalian species such as the purple sea urchin, lamprey, and amphibians. My research focuses on how the immune system evolved outside of mammals and the unique mechanisms that arise under distinct evolutionary pressures.
